Create a Discord bot

Go to the Discord Developer Portal. Click "New Application", give it a name, then navigate to the "Bot" section and click "Add Bot".

Open Discord Developer Portal

Under the Bot section, enable the Message Content Intent toggle (required to read messages).

Paste your bot token

In the Bot section, click "Reset Token" to reveal your bot token. Copy and paste it below.

Add bot to your server

Click the invite link below to add your bot to a Discord server. You need "Manage Server" permission on the target server.

Enter your bot token in the previous step to generate an invite link.

Restrict access (optional)

Limit who can interact with the bot by providing your Discord User ID. Enable Developer Mode in Discord Settings > Advanced, then right-click your name and "Copy User ID".

Comma-separated. Leave blank to allow all server members.
